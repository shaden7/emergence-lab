## 4. Proposed decision (prereg §6)

**All of D-G0 to D-G5 pass.** Under §6 the estimator is *calibrated for*:
- vertex-transitive lattices (exact);
- 2D RGG at k̄ = 12, in windows starting at r = 4 and ending at 2·r_last ≤ 128, with the positive bias quoted above.

It rejects the tested nulls:
- **By the saturation guard (`INSUFFICIENT_RANGE`):** random 4-regular (24/24) and NW p = 1e-2 (8/8).
- **By the plateau criterion (`NO_PLATEAU`):** subdivided binary tree (24/24). Descriptively, also NW p = 1e-4 and 1e-3.

This permits use of the estimator as a Phase-1 instrument with controls. **It says nothing about emergent geometry.** Every graph here has its dimension encoded by construction or is a null.

- **Flagged as fragile (as §5 requires): D-G1.** Its flips are analytic consequences of the §4 table, not sampling effects:
  - At ε = 0.10, Z² L = 256 and all Z³ tori become `NO_PLATEAU`. Z³'s d_eff range over r = 8…32 is 0.113.
  - At r_min = 8, Z¹ L = 1024 and Z² L = 256 have fewer than 3 admissible scales.
  - At guard N/16, Z¹ L = 1024 becomes `INSUFFICIENT_RANGE` and Z³ L ∈ {144, 168} become `NO_PLATEAU`.
  - So ε = 0.15 and r_min = 4 are close to the smallest values at which the smallest lattices pass. Prereg §2 disclosed that ε, r_min and the guard were chosen against this table.
- **D-G2, D-G3 and D-G4 do not flip** under any setting.
  - D-G3's N = 2²¹ mean ranges over 2.0128–2.0172.

## 7. Limitations and counterarguments

- **Single environment.** D-G5 is same-environment determinism only. A GitHub-runner reproduction is outstanding (§1).
- **Two decisive nulls are decided by the guard.** Random 4-regular and NW p = 1e-2 do not exercise the ε criterion. Only the tree tests it among gated nulls. A null that grows slowly and super-polynomially is not in the grid.
- **Coarse estimator.** Dyadic d_eff spans at most a factor of 32 in r here, and the RGG estimate has a small, significant positive bias at k̄ = 12. Other k̄ and 3D RGG are untested (§3 budget).
- **Same model family.** The producer and any later Claude reviewer share a model family. This is AI-mediated checking, not external peer review.

## 8. Evidence level

- **Numerical observations:** the gate outcomes and d̂ values above, for this code, these seeds and this environment.
- **Analytic:** the lattice values and the D-G1 sensitivity flips.
- **Hypotheses:** the cause of the RGG positive bias.
- **Physics claim:** none.
