## 4. Decision (prereg §6; proposed by the producer, accepted after the later-pass checks)

**All of D-G0 to D-G5 pass.** Under §6 the estimator is *calibrated for*:
- vertex-transitive lattices (exact, at the frozen ε = 0.15, r_min = 4 and N/8 guard; at the smaller preregistered lattices the verdict depends on these settings, §5 r1);
- 2D RGG at k̄ = 12, in windows starting at r = 4 and ending at 2·r_last ≤ 128, with the positive bias quoted above.

It rejects the tested nulls:
- **By the saturation guard (`INSUFFICIENT_RANGE`):** random 4-regular (24/24) and NW p = 1e-2 (8/8).
- **By the plateau criterion (`NO_PLATEAU`):** subdivided binary tree (24/24). Descriptively, also NW p = 1e-4 and 1e-3.

This permits use of the estimator as a Phase-1 instrument with controls. **It says nothing about emergent geometry.** Every graph here has its dimension encoded by construction or is a null.

- **Flagged as fragile (as §5 requires): D-G1.** Its flips are analytic consequences of the §4 table, not sampling effects:
  - At ε = 0.10, Z² L = 256 and all Z³ tori become `NO_PLATEAU`. Z³'s d_eff range over r = 8…32 is 0.113.
  - At r_min = 8, Z¹ L = 1024 and Z² L = 256 have fewer than 3 admissible scales.
  - At guard N/16, Z¹ L = 1024 becomes `INSUFFICIENT_RANGE` and Z³ L ∈ {144, 168} become `NO_PLATEAU`.
  - So ε = 0.15 and r_min = 4 are close to the smallest values at which the smallest lattices pass. Prereg §2 disclosed that ε, r_min and the guard were chosen against this table.
- **Prereg §7 falsifier 4 is literally met (r1 disclosure).** Prereg §7 lists "a gate that flips under the mild sensitivity settings" as a falsifier of the method, and D-G1 flips under three of the six §5 settings. This note does not set that aside. It is recorded, with the reading the prereg itself supports:
  - Prereg §5 calls D-G1 "the declared tolerance, not an independent test". Prereg §2 says "the lattice gate therefore checks the implementation, not the choice of ε". The D-G1 flips therefore show that the lattice gate passes only at the frozen ε, r_min and guard. They say nothing new about whether those choices are valid on random graphs.
  - The gates that §2 names as "the actual tests of these choices" (D-G2, D-G3 and D-G4) do not flip under any §5 setting.
  - **Consequence for the decision (§4):** the lattice calibration is exact only for the frozen settings. For Z¹ L = 1024, Z² L = 256 and all three Z³ tori, the `PLATEAU` verdict itself depends on ε, r_min and the guard (recomputed from the stored B̄). A later use of the estimator on lattice-like graphs of comparable size must use the frozen settings and quote this dependence.
  - The other §7 falsifiers are not met. No random 4-regular or subdivided-tree replicate is `PLATEAU` under any §5 setting, and D-G4 never flips. No plateau extends past the crossover: the two ε = 0.20 NW p = 1e-4 windows end at 32 < ξ = 70.7. The RGG drift between 2¹⁹ and 2²¹ is 0.0101 ≤ 0.10.
- **D-G2, D-G3 and D-G4 do not flip** under any setting.
  - D-G3's N = 2²¹ mean ranges over 2.0128–2.0172.
  - At N = 2¹⁷ (not gated), ε = 0.10 leaves only 5/8 `PLATEAU`, and r_min = 8 leaves 0/8.
- **Descriptive flips:** at ε = 0.20, 2/8 NW p = 1e-4 replicates become `PLATEAU` on [4, 32] (d̂ = 2.00, 2.02), with post-window drift 2.36 and 2.22. The window ends at 32 < ξ. At r_min = 2, NW p = 1e-2 becomes `NO_PLATEAU` instead of `INSUFFICIENT_RANGE`.

## 7. Limitations and counterarguments

- **Single environment.** D-G5 is same-environment determinism only. A GitHub-runner reproduction is outstanding (§1).
- **Method falsifier 4 (prereg §7) is met by D-G1** (§5 r1). By the prereg's own reading, D-G1 is an implementation check. The random-graph gates do not flip. The lattice calibration is therefore scoped to the frozen settings.
- **Two decisive nulls are decided by the guard.** Random 4-regular and NW p = 1e-2 do not exercise the ε criterion. Only the tree tests it among gated nulls. A null that grows slowly and super-polynomially is not in the grid.
- **Coarse estimator.** Dyadic d_eff spans at most a factor of 32 in r here, and the RGG estimate has a small, significant positive bias at k̄ = 12. Other k̄ and 3D RGG are untested (§3 budget).
- **Same model family.** The producer and any later Claude reviewer share a model family. This is AI-mediated checking, not external peer review.
