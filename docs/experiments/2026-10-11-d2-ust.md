## Descriptive (not gated)

- **Pooled curve over 2048 sources:** `PLATEAU` at every size. d̂ is 1.562 at L = 256 (window 8–256), 1.560 at L = 512 (8–512) and 1.550 at L = 1024 (16–1024). Pooled d_eff at L = 1024 is 1.34 (r = 4), 1.44 (8), 1.52 (16), 1.56 (32), 1.58 (64), 1.61 (128), 1.60 (256), 1.55 (512).
- **Level:** every size's replicate mean and every pooled d̂ lie 0.03–0.05 *below* 8/5. The t-intervals at L = 256 and 1024 contain 1.6; the L = 512 interval, [1.547, 1.573], does not. *Hypothesis, not tested:* a finite-size or torus correction to the dyadic ratio, of the kind §4 says the theorem does not bound. The prereg tolerance (0.15) was fixed before any output and is met with room. The d_eff values at r ≤ 8 rise toward 1.6 and are outside the plateau windows.
- **Sensitivity:** ε ∈ {0.10, 0.20}, r_min ∈ {2, 8} and guard fractions 1/16 and 1/4 cause **no verdict flips** in any of the 24 replicates. So, unlike CAY (where ε = 0.10 flips 2/8 at 2²²), G2 is not fragile under the mild sensitivity settings.
- **Scatter:** the per-replicate sd(d̂) is 0.054, 0.016 and 0.049 at the three sizes. §5a gave no UST power model, so there is no reference value. The sd at L = 512 is about a third of the other two. With 8 replicates the χ² interval of a sample sd is wide (about 0.66× to 2.0× the estimate), so this is not singled out as an effect.
- **Separation from the ambient lattice value 2:** the largest replicate d̂ over all 24 is 1.654, and every interval ends below 1.61. The instrument tracks the tree's intrinsic exponent, not the embedding dimension of the torus.

## Interpretation and limitations

- *Numerical observation:* on the uniform spanning tree of the 2D torus, whose intrinsic volume-growth exponent 8/5 is proved up to constants [BM11] and is not encoded in the construction, the frozen D1 estimator returns `PLATEAU` at all sizes. d̂ is 1.567 ± 0.041 (95 %) at L = 1024, stable across sizes, insensitive to the mild settings, and clearly separated from 2.
- With CAY, two of the three D2 families now pass. The §6 decision still needs G3 (RRT null, which tests the saturation guard). This note makes no §6 claim.
- The consistent ≈ 0.04 downward offset is within tolerance. It means a later application should not read d̂ more finely than about ±0.05 on graphs of this kind and size.
- §4 already notes that volume-growth dimension is not spectral dimension (16/13 for the 2D UST). Nothing here bears on spectral or physical dimension.
- The run is in a single environment; there is no cross-environment hash. Producer, CAY reviewers and this run are the same model family. The checks above are the producer's own and are not independent.
