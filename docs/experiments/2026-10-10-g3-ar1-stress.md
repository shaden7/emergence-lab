# G3 addendum — AR(1) high-φ stress test of the production ESS estimator

## Preregistration (committed before the first execution of `scripts/g3_ar1_stress.py`)

**Why.** Report gate G3 (`docs/ISING_CALIBRATION_REPORT.md` §D) asks for "robust τ/ESS window sensitivity and marking short chains as insufficient, **including AR1 φ ≥ 0.98**". The φ ≥ 0.98 stress test in PR #12 (`scripts/autocorr_synthetic_audit.py`) exercised the *old* heuristic in `stats.py`. Gates 4 and 5 and the L32 gate rely on the *newer* Geyer estimator in `src/emergence_lab/convergence.py`, whose unit tests only cover φ = 0.5 and 0.9. This addendum closes that literal gap before the Phase-0 GO/NO-GO decision. It is a methods check of a known estimator on a process with an exactly known answer; no physics claim.

**Process.** Stationary Gaussian AR(1), x₀ ~ N(0, 1/(1−φ²)), unit innovations. Exact integrated autocorrelation time in the convention used by `convergence.py` (τ = 1 + 2Σρ_k): τ = (1+φ)/(1−φ), i.e. **τ = 99 at φ = 0.98** and **τ = 399 at φ = 0.995**.

**Design (fixed).** 16 independent chains per cell, `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)` per cell, seeds 2031100001 (φ 0.98 long), 2031100002 (φ 0.995 long), 2031100003 (φ 0.98 short), 2031100004 (φ 0.995 short). Long chains n = 500 τ (49,500 / 199,500 draws); short chains n = 20 τ (1,980 / 7,980 draws). Budget: < 4·10⁶ draws in total, seconds of CPU; no runner, no Lightsail.

**Criteria (all must hold for PASS).**
- **A1 (accuracy, multi-chain):** long cells, `ess(chains)["tau"]` within ±15 % of the exact τ.
- **A2 (accuracy, per chain):** long cells, median over 16 chains of `chain_ess(x)["tau"]` within ±20 % of exact τ.
- **A3 (no gross per-chain underestimate):** long cells, at most 2 of 16 chains with `chain_ess` τ̂ < 0.5 τ.
- **S1 (short chains flagged, per chain):** short cells, **all** 16 chains have `chain_ess` ESS < 100 (the per-chain threshold of the L32 gate G3) or undefined.
- **S2 (short chains flagged, multi-chain):** short cells, `ess_bulk` < 400 (the bulk threshold of the L32 gate G2).

Expected statistical scatter: for a truncation window of a few τ, the relative SD of a single-chain τ̂ is ≈ √(2(2M+1)/n) ≈ 0.2 at n = 500 τ, so A2 (median of 16) has ≈ 3σ room and A1 more. S1/S2 have nominal ESS ≈ 20 per chain and 320 total, so they fail only if τ is underestimated by a factor ≳ 5 (S1) or if the multi-chain estimate exceeds 1.25× nominal (S2).

**Interpretation rule.** PASS → G3's AR(1) clause is met for `convergence.py` in this regime. Any FAIL is retained as a negative result and **blocks a Phase-0 GO** until the estimator or its use is repaired; criteria are not to be changed after execution. Descriptive (non-gated) output: short-chain τ̂/τ ratios.
