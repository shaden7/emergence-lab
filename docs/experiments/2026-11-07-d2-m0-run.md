## Interpretation (hypothesis level only)

- *Hypothesis:* in finite-N MM estimates averaged over small intervals of a D=2 sprinkling
  without endpoints, apparent **reduction** below 2 can arise from estimator/selection effects
  alone at N≤3, while averaging biases **upward** for 4≤N≤32. A short-scale reduction claim
  built on averaged MM dimensions at very small N would therefore need this reference as a
  control. Whether the same pattern holds for endpoint-conditioned intervals, D=3/4, other
  estimators, or real CS0 data is untested.
- No fit, no scaling law and no claim about the guiding question (Issue #37) are made.

## Limitations
