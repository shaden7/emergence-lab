## Interpretation (hypotheses, not results)

- The NO-GO is driven by P5, as anticipated in prereg §8: eight trees per size cannot establish ±0.03 equivalence (UST), and CAY shows a size dependence of +0.035 to +0.071 at fixed t. One possible explanation is a finite-N effect in the annealed mean (e.g. a heavier contribution of short dead ends or of the local structure around a uniform source at smaller N); another is ordinary sampling error across correlated windows. Neither is tested here.
- Pilot slopes stay below the asymptotic targets for UST (≈ 1.19–1.21 vs 16/13 ≈ 1.231) and drift through 4/3 for CAY. With the observed drift and size dependence, a two-family discrimination of the 0.103 gap with this estimator at T ≤ 4096 is not supported by this pilot.

## Limitations
