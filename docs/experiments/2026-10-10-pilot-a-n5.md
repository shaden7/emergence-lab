## Experiment and analytical null

Exactly 4 models × 3 σ values (0, 1e-6, 1e-4) × 3 thresholds (1e-3, 1e-6, 1e-9) × 20 independent detector realisations × 7 *development-only* measurement sites = **5,040 recorded site readings** from 720 distinct keyed random streams. Models W/H/Q at t=1 and CA at n=2; site grid `[0,1,2,4,6,8,12]`; the original spatial metric and clock are **inputs**, not inferred quantities.

Each detector obtains one noisy observation per arm, `S_hat = |S_true + ε_do − ε_control|`, with ε values independent N(0,σ²) by assumption. Analytically zero sites in the W and CA support exterior must therefore have probability `p_false = erfc(eta/(2σ))` (σ>0); σ=0 implies zero false positives. Positive H and Q tails may be **undetected** yet remain positive mathematically.

Intervals are descriptive and marginal, with no multiple-comparison adjustment, not confidence about fundamental physics.

The adversarial **measured-front result** is especially telling. In strict W at σ=η=1e-6, the farthest detected site was r=12 in **6/20** replicates (and r=8 in 10/20), versus the noise-free grid front r=1. In strict CA at the same parameters, r=12 was detected in **4/20**, r=8 in **8/20**, versus noise-free front r=2. Across all theorem-exterior sites, at least one spurious signal was seen in **20/20 W** and **18/20 CA** independent replicates for this condition. These apparent distant fronts are **detector artifacts**, not propagation beyond strict model support.

**Results at sigma=0:** W front r=1, CA front r=2 for all thresholds/realisations; H r=4/6/8 and Q r=4/6/6 for thresholds 1e-3/1e-6/1e-9. These are sparse, input-grid-based diagnostics and never strict H/Q cones.
