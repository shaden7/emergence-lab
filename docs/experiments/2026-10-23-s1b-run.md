## Descriptive comparison with the S1 pilot (post hoc, not a preregistered test)

The pilot's CAY contrast was +0.0706 (SE 0.0289, 8 trees/size); S1b gives −0.0433 (SE 0.0274,
24 trees/size). The naive difference, 0.114 with combined SE ≈ 0.040 (≈ 2.9 SE), is larger
than both runs' nominal errors suggest. The per-size slopes moved in opposite directions
(2¹⁸: 1.378 → 1.293; 2¹⁶: 1.307 → 1.337). Possible readings, none tested here: the pilot
estimate was inflated by the data-informed window choice (winner's curse, flagged in the
prereg); tree-to-tree heavy tails make the 8-tree pilot SE too optimistic; or chance. Samples
are not pooled, per the prereg. The CAY 2¹⁴ cohort does not show a monotone size trend
(1.302, 1.337, 1.293).

## Limitations

- Welch/delta-method levels are nominal and uncalibrated for heavy-tailed annealed walks; the
  reliability screen passing does not validate coverage.
- New seeds cannot reveal a defect shared with the pilot (generators, kernel, stratification,
  large-t truncation).
- The ±0.03 margin is an engineering convention.
- At D ≈ +0.035 the design had ~8 % power, so effects just above the margin are not excluded by
  this run. The observed CI does, however, sit almost entirely below +0.03.
- The single run's verdict is final for S1b. No rerun, no pooling, and no S2 from this result.
