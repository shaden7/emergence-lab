## Descriptive (not gated)

- **Replicate mean against the mean-curve d̂ (§4):** 1.9439 against 1.9290 at 2²² (+0.015), and 1.8981 against 1.9247 at 2²⁰ (−0.027). Both are well inside the interval.
- **Pooled curve over 2048 sources:** `PLATEAU` at every size. d̂ is 1.859 at 2¹⁸ (window 16–256), 1.852 at 2²⁰ (16–512) and 1.890 at 2²² (16–1024). At 2²² the pooled B̄(1024) and B̄(2048) are 4.8 % and 4.7 % below the exact means, so the pooled d̂ is 0.039 below the exact mean-curve value at the same r_last. At these radii the ball sizes do not self-average across 8 trees. At 2²², G0 tests only r ≤ 512, where |t| ≤ 1.15.
- **Per-replicate scatter is larger than the §5a model:** sd(d̂) at 2²² is 0.085, against the model's 0.051 (≈ 1.7×). With 8 replicates the 95 % χ² interval for this sd is roughly [0.056, 0.173], so it excludes 0.051. *Hypothesis, not tested:* the within-tree design effect at 2²² is larger than the pilot curve extrapolated from 2¹⁴–2¹⁷. The §5a figure P(G1) ≈ 0.997 should therefore be treated as optimistic. The prereg already lists this extrapolation as an assumption. The UST power, which was never modelled, is the more exposed case.
- **Sensitivity:** ε = 0.10 changes `PLATEAU` to `NO_PLATEAU` in 1/8 replicates at 2¹⁸, 1/8 at 2²⁰ and **2/8 at 2²²**. Under ε = 0.10 the 8/8 condition of G1 would therefore fail, so **G1 is fragile under ε = 0.10**, as D-G1 was in D1. ε = 0.20, r_min ∈ {2, 8} and the guards N/4 and N/16 cause no flips, and the pooled curve is `PLATEAU` under every setting.

## Interpretation and limitations

- *Numerical observation:* on uniform labelled trees (intrinsic volume-growth dimension 2, Hausdorff dimension of the CRT), the frozen D1 estimator returns `PLATEAU` with d̂ within 0.06 of 2 at N = 2²², is stable between 2²⁰ and 2²², and the generator and BFS match exact mathematics.
- Calibration on CAY alone does not make the instrument calibrated for non-embedded graphs. §6 requires G2 (UST, non-integer 1.6) and G3 (RRT null) as well.
- The ε = 0.10 fragility and the larger-than-modelled scatter mean that this pass relies on the frozen ε = 0.15. That is legitimate under the prereg but should be weighed in the §6 decision.
- The run is in a single environment; no cross-environment hash exists. The producer is the same model family as the earlier reviewers.
